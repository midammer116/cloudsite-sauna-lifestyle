How to Sauna – A Beginner's Guide

A clear, friendly guide to your first sauna session, from showering and sitting to cooling down and hydrating.

How to Sauna – A Beginner's Guide is about bringing the warmth, rhythm and calm of the Finnish sauna into your everyday life. On this page we explore what it really means, how to make it work for your routine, and the small habits that turn a good sweat into a lasting lifestyle.

Before you enter

When it comes to before you enter, small, consistent choices count far more than intensity. The people who make sauna part of their life do not chase the longest or hottest session — they build a routine that is enjoyable enough to repeat, and gentle enough to sustain over the long run.

Start by thinking about what you actually want from this part of the practice: relaxation, recovery, clearer thinking, or simply a quiet moment to yourself. With that intention in mind, a few practical steps are all it takes to make before you enter feel natural rather than forced.

Keep it simple and repeatable

The best sauna habits are the ones you do not have to negotiate with yourself about. Pick a regular time, keep the session short enough to look forward to, and pair it with a small ritual you enjoy — a glass of water, a minute of quiet, or a stretch. Over time the repetition becomes the reward.

Enjoy Your First Session

We hope this guide to How to Sauna – A Beginner's Guide gives you a clear, practical starting point. The Finnish sauna is not about extremes — it is about consistency, listening to your body, and carving out a space where you can slow down. Start small, be kind to yourself, and let the habit build itself.
