Our Approach to Privacy

Sauna Lifestyle is a static informational website. We believe in being transparent and keeping data collection to an absolute minimum. This page explains what information is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not operate accounts, sell products directly, or ask you to create a profile on this website. As a result, we do not collect personal data such as your name, email address, or contact details through this site.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools that may run in the background may process technical information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. This is used only for standard traffic measurement and to keep the site reliable.

Embedded Content

Some pages embed content from third-party services, including YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. When you load a page containing these embeds, the relevant third party may set cookies and collect usage data under their own privacy policies. Please review those providers' policies for details on how they handle your information.

Our Use of Data

Any aggregated, anonymised traffic data we may see is used solely to understand which content is helpful and to improve the site. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Your Choices

You can browse this website with your browser's privacy settings, use a private or incognito window, or disable cookies. If you have questions about your privacy on this site, please contact us through our contact page.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, along with an updated effective date. This policy was last updated in 2026.
